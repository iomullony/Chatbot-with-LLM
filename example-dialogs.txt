DIALOG 1: Incoming Exchange Student - Application Deadlines & Process
Target: International Office (Incomings & Deadlines)
======================================================================
USER: Hello! I want to come to DIT as an exchange student for the winter semester. When is the application period?
ASSISSTANT: For the winter semester (October entries), the application period runs from April 1st to June 1st. The online application portal is only activated during this time.
USER: Can I just go ahead and fill out the application form on your website right now?
ASSISSTANT: No, you must first be officially nominated by your home university. Once they nominate you, you will receive a link to the online application form.
USER: Do I need to mail my physical documents to Deggendorf after completing the form?
ASSISSTANT: No, nothing should be sent by post or email. The entire process is done online via a system called Mobility Online, where you will upload documents like your CV, photo, passport, and grade sheet.

DIALOG 2: Exchange Programmes & Language Requirements
Target: International Office (Programmes & Requirements)
======================================================================
USER: Hi, I'm a Computer Science student. Are there any specific English-taught programmes available for exchange students on the Deggendorf campus?
ASSISSTANT: Yes, DIT offers the International Computer Science programme for business Bachelor students, which is 100% in English and free of charge. Please note it is not available for free movers due to limited capacity.
USER: What are the language proficiency requirements to qualify for this exchange?
ASSISSTANT: You must have a good to excellent knowledge of the language of instruction (English or German) at a minimum level of B1. 
USER: Do I need to submit an official language certificate like TOEFL or IELTS for admission?
ASSISSTANT: No, language certificates are not strictly required for your admission to the exchange programme.

DIALOG 3: Language Centre & TestDaF Fees
Target: International Office / Language Centre
======================================================================
USER: Hello! Are exchange students required to learn German while studying at DIT?
ASSISSTANT: Yes, all exchange students are required to participate in German language courses during their semester. DIT offers free German language courses from levels A1 to C1.
USER: Can I take an official language exam like the TestDaF at the university?
ASSISSTANT: Yes, DIT is a licensed examination centre for TestDaF. The examination fee for the TestDaF is €210.
USER: Whom should I contact if I want to register for the exam?
ASSISSTANT: You should contact the Language Centre directly by writing an email to sprachenzentrum@th-deg.de.

DIALOG 4: Orientation Week & Arrival Support
Target: International Office (Arrival & Orientation)
======================================================================
USER: Hi, I am planning my arrival for the upcoming semester. Is the Orientation Week mandatory?
ASSISSTANT: Yes, Orientation Week is a compulsory event for all exchange students at the main campus in Deggendorf. It takes place one to two weeks before the semester starts, annually in March and September.
USER: What kind of activities are organized during this week?
ASSISSTANT: Activities include a guided campus and town tour, IT and library introductions, intercultural training, a Bavarian Evening, day trips, and help registering at the residents' registration office.
USER: Who can I email if I have specific questions about incoming exchange logistics?
ASSISSTANT: You can contact the incoming exchange student team directly at incomings@th-deg.de.
