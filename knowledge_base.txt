# THD/DIT International Office - Knowledge Base

The International Office at Deggendorf Institute of Technology (DIT), also known as Technische Hochschule Deggendorf (THD), is the central contact point for all questions about studying abroad and incoming international students. 

The International Office advises students who wish to spend a semester abroad, supports international students and scholarship holders, maintains European partnerships within the Erasmus+ programme, and coordinates university partnerships worldwide.

The correct email address to contact the International Office for incoming exchange students is incomings@th-deg.de. 

The general International Office email is international-office@th-deg.de. 

The university's main address is Deggendorf Institute of Technology, Dieter-Görlitz-Platz 1, 94469 Deggendorf, Germany. 

The main phone number is +49 991 3615-0.

DIT has three campuses: the main campus in Deggendorf, the European Campus Rottal-Inn (ECRI) in Pfarrkirchen, and the Cham Teaching and Research Campus. 

Almost 50% of DIT students come from over 120 countries. DIT is considered Bavaria's most international university of applied sciences and has the largest range of English-language degree programmes in Bavaria. 

The European Campus Rottal-Inn is the only campus in Bavaria where all lectures are held exclusively in English.

DIT offers several English-taught exchange programmes for incoming students. On the Deggendorf campus these include: General Business (for business Bachelor students), General Engineering (for engineering Bachelor students), International Computer Science (for computer science Bachelor students, not available for free movers due to limited capacity), and General Health (for healthcare sciences Bachelor students). All these programmes are 100% in English and are free of charge for Bachelor students. Students can earn up to 30 ECTS credits per semester. The winter semester starts on 1 October and the summer semester starts on 15 March.

The ERASMUS+ study semester with work experience programme allows European exchange students to combine a study semester at DIT with a practical internship in Germany. After the study semester, students complete a single-semester internship directly at DIT or at a partnering German company. DIT supports students with German language courses, intercultural training, and soft skill workshops, and helps with VISA and work permits. 

As a European exchange student, you can apply for an Erasmus+ grant. If funds are available, the International Office may also provide an additional scholarship — students should clarify this with the International Office beforehand.

The application periods for exchange students are: 1 April to 1 June for October (winter semester) entries, and 1 October to 1 December for March (summer semester) entries. Applications are only possible during these periods, as the online application portal is only activated then. 

Exchange students must be officially nominated by their home university before receiving the application link. 

Free movers (students from non-partner universities) can apply only upon request.

The application process is done entirely online via a system called Mobility Online. 

After being nominated by your home university, you will receive a link to the online application form. You then set a password and upload the required documents, which include a CV, a photo, a passport or national ID card, a grade sheet, and a certificate of enrolment. Nothing should be sent by post or email — everything is done through Mobility Online.

To qualify for an exchange programme at DIT, students must have completed at least two semesters at their home university and have a good to excellent knowledge of the language of instruction (English or German), at least level B1. 

Prior knowledge of German is not required but is recommended. Language certificates are not required for admission.

All exchange students are required to participate in German language courses during their semester. 

DIT offers free German language courses at levels A1 to C1. 

An intensive German course at A1 level is also offered during Orientation Week. 

Students can obtain official German language certificates at DIT, including TestDaF and telc exams. 

DIT is a licensed examination centre for TestDaF. The TestDaF exam fee is €210. To register, students should contact the Language Centre at sprachenzentrum@th-deg.de.

The Orientation Week is a compulsory event for all exchange students at the main campus in Deggendorf. 

The Orientation Week takes place one to two weeks before the start of every semester, annually in March and September. 
Activities in The Orientation Week include a guided campus and town tour, IT and library introduction, registering at the residents' registration office, obtaining a residence permit, intercultural training, a Bavarian Evening, and various day trips. 

At the European Campus Rottal-Inn, an Orientation Day is held instead, which covers course information, help with town hall registration, health insurance, opening a bank account, and obtaining a student card.

The Host Family Programme connects local Bavarian families with international students. Families invite students to dinner or on day trips, giving students an insight into Bavarian family life. Application periods are: Deggendorf — 1 March to 15 April and 15 September to 31 October; Cham and Pfarrkirchen — 15 September to 31 October. Applications during the semester are not possible. Deggendorf students should contact marion.loefflmann@th-deg.de; ECRI students should contact andrea.ebertseder@th-deg.de; Cham students should contact zeynep.evli@th-deg.de.

The International Week is an annual event at DIT that brings together students, staff, and guests from around the world to promote cultural exchange. 

Key events of The International Week include "Taste the World", where international students share traditional dishes from their home countries, and the Erzählcafé, an intercultural storytelling café held in cooperation with the Kulturviertel Deggendorf. 

The Erzählcafé encourages intercultural communication across all nationalities and age groups. Registration is required as places are limited; contact erzaehlcafe@th-deg.de or register at office B 210.

DIT offers an International Mentoring Programme for international students. 

There is also a buddy system and support for finding accommodation. 

The Studentenwerk operates student dormitories. 

Students are advised to apply for a dormitory place early as spots are limited. Private housing is also available in Deggendorf.

For non-EU students, visa and residence permit information is provided by the International Office. 

Non-EU students typically need a student visa before arriving in Germany. After arrival, they must register at the local residents' registration office (Einwohnermeldeamt) and apply for a residence permit. DIT's Orientation Week includes help with these formalities.

Credit recognition after an exchange is handled by the responsible examination office in cooperation with the International Office. Students must complete a Learning Agreement before their stay, listing the courses they plan to take at DIT. This document must be signed by the student, the home university, and DIT. Courses listed in the Learning Agreement that are passed at DIT are generally recognised as equivalent credits at the home university.

DIT is located in Deggendorf, Bavaria, a town on the Danube river at the gateway to the Bavarian Forest. 

Students in Deggendorf can easily reach nearby cities like Passau, Regensburg, and Munich by train. 

Leisure activities around include horseback riding, canoeing on the Danube, hiking in the Bavarian Forest, and skiing in winter. 

The cost of living in Deggendorf is lower compared to larger German cities like Munich or Berlin.
