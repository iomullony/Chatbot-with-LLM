# THD/DIT International Office - Knowledge Base
# Each section separated by a blank line is treated as one retrievable chunk.

The International Office at Deggendorf Institute of Technology (DIT), also known as Technische Hochschule Deggendorf (THD), is the central contact point for all questions about studying abroad and incoming exchange students. It is located on the main campus in Deggendorf, Bavaria, Germany.

DIT is a member of the Erasmus+ program, which allows students to study at partner universities in Europe for one or two semesters. Students can receive an Erasmus grant to help cover living costs abroad. The grant amount depends on the destination country.

To apply for an Erasmus+ exchange, students must first find a partner university from DIT's list of Erasmus partners. Applications are submitted through the International Office, typically in the semester before the planned exchange. Required documents usually include a transcript of records, a motivation letter, and a language certificate.

The application deadline for Erasmus+ exchanges for the winter semester is usually in February or March. For the summer semester, the deadline is typically in September or October. Students should check the International Office website for the exact deadlines each year.

DIT also offers non-European exchange opportunities through bilateral agreements with universities in Asia, North America, and Australia. The application process and deadlines for these partnerships may differ from the Erasmus+ process.

Incoming international students who want to study at DIT can apply through the international application portal. Most programs at DIT are taught in German, but there are some English-taught programs and modules available, particularly in the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering.

Language requirements for incoming students depend on the program. German-taught programs typically require at least B2 level German. English-taught programs require proof of English proficiency, such as IELTS or TOEFL scores.

The International Office offers a buddy program that pairs incoming international students with local DIT students. The buddy helps new students get settled, navigate campus, and find their way around Deggendorf.

DIT provides support for students with finding accommodation. The Studentenwerk operates student dormitories near the campus. Students are advised to apply for a dormitory place early, as spots are limited. Private housing is also available in Deggendorf.

The International Office can be reached by email at international@th-deg.de or by phone during office hours. Walk-in consultations are available on specific days; students should check the current schedule on the DIT website.

Students planning to study abroad need to complete a Learning Agreement before their stay. This document lists the courses they plan to take at the host university and must be signed by the student, DIT, and the host university. It ensures that credits earned abroad are recognized at DIT.

Credit recognition after an exchange is handled by the responsible examination office in cooperation with the International Office. Courses listed in the Learning Agreement that were passed at the host university are generally recognized as equivalent credits at DIT.

The Faculty of Computer Science at DIT offers Bachelor programs in Computer Science (Informatik), Applied Computer Science, and a Master program in Computer Science. There is also an international English-taught Master program in Artificial Intelligence.

Prospective students interested in studying at DIT can attend information events organized by the International Office, including online info sessions and open days. These events are listed on the DIT website.

Visa and residence permit information for non-EU students is provided by the International Office. Non-EU students typically need a student visa before arriving in Germany. After arrival, they must register with the local residents' registration office (Einwohnermeldeamt) and apply for a residence permit.
